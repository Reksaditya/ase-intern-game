## Freesound sounds

Freesound does not provide one blanket license for every sound. Each uploader
selects a license for each sound. Freesound's current FAQ describes CC0, CC BY
4.0, and CC BY-NC 4.0; older sounds may also carry the retired Sampling+ terms.
Check each sound's own Freesound page and preserve its exact license and
attribution requirements. In general, CC BY requires attribution, CC BY-NC
does not permit commercial use, and CC0 does not require attribution under the
license. These are summaries only; the individual license text controls.

References:

- [Freesound FAQ: licenses and attribution](https://freesound.org/help/faq/)
- [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

## itch.io assets and sounds

itch.io is a distribution platform, not a single asset license. The itch.io
Terms of Service make publishers responsible for having the rights needed to
distribute their content; they do not give this project blanket rights to
third-party asset packs. Follow the license supplied by the specific asset
creator/listing, including any limits on commercial use, attribution,
modification, or redistribution in a source repository or game build.

References:

- [itch.io Terms of Service](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms)
- [itch.io Creator FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq)
